Today's global celebrations

One planet, a thousand reasons to celebrate.

Cultural Compass maps the world's holidays, festivals, and quiet observances onto a single calendar — so you always know who's celebrating what, and why.

Why this exists

Most calendars only show you your own country's holidays. Cultural Compass was built to widen that view: every date carries stories from dozens of cultures at once, from national independence days to religious festivals to days of quiet remembrance.

We believe understanding what other people hold sacred, joyful, or important is a small, steady way to build cultural awareness — one date at a time.

How to use it

Switch between Month, Week, and Day views to explore the calendar at your own pace. Tap any highlighted date to see everything being celebrated that day, anywhere in the world. Use the compass icon in the top bar to browse holidays by country.
